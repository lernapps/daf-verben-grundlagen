Context: the page “Imperative (du, ihr, Sie)”

Give instructions, tips, and polite requests using the imperative for du, ihr, and Sie — verb in Position 1.

Tips and Instructions in Lindenberg

Just like in yes/no questions, the verb stands in Position 1 in the imperative. Depending on who you address, German has three forms: du, ihr, and Sie.

  • Exercise imperativ-einstieg (below)

How to Form the Three Imperatives

Imperative for du, ihr, and Sie:

  • Infinitive | du / ihr (no pronoun) | Sie (keep Sie!)
  • kommen (come) | Komm! / Kommt! | Kommen Sie!
  • arbeiten (work) | Arbeite! / Arbeitet! | Arbeiten Sie!
  • sprechen (e → i) | Sprich! / Sprecht! | Sprechen Sie!
  • lesen (e → ie) | Lies! / Lest! | Lesen Sie!
  • fahren (a → ä) | Fahr! / Fahrt! | Fahren Sie!
  • sein (irregular) | Sei! / Seid! | Seien Sie!

Remember: Golden Rule for the du-Imperative

Take the du-form and drop -st and du: du kommst → Komm! Keep e → i/ie (Sprich!, Lies!), but drop the umlaut a → ä (Fahr!, Schlaf!). After -t/-d, keep -e: Arbeite!

Exercise imperativ-einstieg

  • Task: Herr Braun tells the whole class: 'Öffnet bitte das Buch auf Seite 10!' Who is he addressing?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ several people informally (ihr) – Correct: The ihr-imperative uses the regular ihr-form (ihr öffnet) without the pronoun ihr.
    • ✗ one single student (du) – For du it would be: Öffne bitte das Buch!
    • ✗ one adult formally (Sie) – The Sie-imperative always keeps the pronoun Sie: Öffnen Sie bitte das Buch!
  • Solution: several people informally (ihr)
  • Explanation: In German the verb form immediately shows whether you address one peer (du), a group (ihr), or someone formally (Sie).

Exercise imperativ-zuordnen

  • Task: Match each imperative form to the right person.
  • Answer format: match each item on the left with one on the right
  • Solution: Komm bitte mit! – du (1 friend); Kommt bitte mit! – ihr (several friends); Kommen Sie bitte mit! – Sie (formal); Sei bitte leise! – du (irregular form of sein)
  • Explanation: Komm (du), Kommt (ihr), Kommen Sie (formal), and Sei (special du-form of sein).

Exercise imperativ-wahl

  • Task: Choose the right du-imperative form.
  • Answer format: choose a word for each gap
  • How the app counts mistakes of form: capital letters wrong, accents wrong, hyphen wrong, apostrophe wrong, punctuation wrong
  • Items (start → solution):
  1. Speak slowly, please! (sprechen, du) → Sprich bitte langsam!
  2. Ride carefully! (fahren, du) → Fahr vorsichtig!
  3. Read the sentence! (lesen, du) → Lies den Satz!
  4. Be punctual! (sein, du) → Sei pünktlich!
  • Explanation: e → i/ie stays in the du-imperative (Sprich!, Lies!), a → ä drops its umlaut (Fahr!), and sein has the special form Sei!.
  • How to help: the app checks word by word against the solutions above and names mistakes of form (e.g. accents) with the child's words. Do not give the solution: ask about the rule (ending, gender, tense) or about a similar sentence of the page. If the app takes a version of the child's as wrong that you think is right, tell the child: the app knows only the versions above.

Exercise imperativ-luecken

  • Task: Form the right imperative for du, ihr, or Sie.
  • Answer format: fill in the gaps in German
  • How the app counts mistakes of form: capital letters right with a hint, accents wrong, hyphen wrong, apostrophe wrong, punctuation wrong
  • Items (start → solution):
  1. Frau Weber to Peter (du): Eat a roll! (essen) → Iss ein Brötchen, Peter!
    • Ess: With essen (e → i), keep the i in the du-imperative: Iss!
  2. Herr Braun to the class (ihr): Ask Nadia! (fragen) → Fragt bitte Nadia!
    • Fragen: The ihr-imperative takes the -t ending of ihr fragt: Fragt!
  3. Peter to a neighbor (Sie): Please wait a moment! (warten) → Warten Sie bitte einen Moment!
    • Warten: In the formal imperative, always include Sie right after the verb: Warten Sie!
  • Explanation: Well done! You now master Position 2 (statements, W-questions) and Position 1 (yes/no questions, imperatives). In the final chapter, we add the right bracket and Position 0.

Do not give the solution: ask about the rule (ending, gender, tense) or about a similar sentence of the page. If the app takes a version of the child's as wrong that you think is right, tell the child: the app knows only the versions above.
