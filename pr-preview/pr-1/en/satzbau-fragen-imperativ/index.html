Verb-Second Order, Questions and Imperative

Using the Sentence Cards and the fixed Position-2 Verb Anchor, you build statements, W-questions, yes/no questions, and simple instructions in the imperative.

  1. Verb-Second Word Order Build German statements with the conjugated verb in second position, including inversion after time or place.
  2. Asking and Answering W-Questions Ask and answer W-questions with wer, was, wo, woher, wohin, wann, and wie — keeping the verb in Position 2.
  3. Yes/No Questions and doch Form yes/no questions with the conjugated verb in Position 1 and answer with ja, nein, or doch.
  4. Imperative (du, ihr, Sie) Give instructions, tips, and polite requests using the imperative for du, ihr, and Sie — verb in Position 1.
