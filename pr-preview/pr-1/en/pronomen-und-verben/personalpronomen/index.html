Arriving at the Weber Home

Sam arrives in Lindenberg as an exchange student. Frau Weber, Lukas (15), and Mina (12) wait at the station. Which pronoun fits the first greeting?

Exercise

Sam speaks to Frau Weber first. She is an adult he does not know well yet. Which pronoun does he use?

  • Sie (capitalized) right answer

    Correct: With adults you address formally, German uses capitalized Sie.

  • du

    du is informal singular, used for peers, children, friends, and family — like Lukas and Mina.

  • ihr

    ihr addresses two or more people informally (like 'you all').

German distinguishes informal you (du singular, ihr plural) from formal you (Sie, always capitalized, for one or more people).

The Subject Card at a Glance

A nominative personal pronoun sits on the Subject Card: it tells you who does the action. Watch the three uses of sie / Sie.

Nominative personal pronouns
PronounMeaningExample in Lindenberg
ichIIch bin Sam.
duyou (singular, informal)Du bist Lukas.
er / sie / eshe / she / itEr ist 15. Sie ist 12. Es ist kalt.
wirweWir wohnen in Lindenberg.
ihryou all (plural, informal)Ihr spielt Fußball.
sietheySie kommen aus Lindenberg.
Sieyou (formal, sg. & pl.)Sind Sie Frau Weber?

Watch out: sie, sie, Sie

sie means she or they — the verb ending tells you which one. Capitalized Sie is always formal you.

Practice

Exercise

Match each person or group in Lindenberg to the right subject pronoun.

Choose the match for each entry.

Right answer: Mina (12) – sie (she); Lukas and I (Sam) – wir; Lukas and Mina (Sam talks to both) – ihr; Herr Braun (Sam's teacher, direct address) – Sie

Mina is feminine singular (sie), Lukas + I is wir, two peers addressed together are ihr, and a teacher is addressed formally as Sie.

Exercise

Choose the right personal pronoun for each sentence.

  1. Sam talks about Lukas: He is fifteen years old. → … ist fünfzehn Jahre alt.
  2. Sam talks to Lukas and Mina: You all live in Lindenberg. → … wohnt in Lindenberg.
  3. Sam talks about Frau Weber and Herr Weber: They are very friendly. → … sind sehr freundlich.
  4. Sam talks about himself and Lukas: We go to school. → … gehen zur Schule.
Solution
  1. Er ist fünfzehn Jahre alt.
  2. Ihr wohnt in Lindenberg.
  3. Sie sind sehr freundlich.
  4. Wir gehen zur Schule.

Always check who is on the Subject Card: one person (er/sie/es), yourself plus others (wir), peers addressed directly (ihr), or other people in the plural (sie).
